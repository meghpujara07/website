Empty hours don't show up anywhere

A diary tells you who booked. It never tells you who didn't, which hours keep failing, or whether the turf across town is doing better with the same slot.

Idle hours stay invisible

Weekday mornings and late nights go unsold for months. Nobody notices until the monthly total comes in low and the reason is already gone.

The real take is unclear

Cash at the gate, online transfers and whatever staff collected all land in one pile. Revenue per turf stays an estimate.

Turfs can't be compared

When one site fills its 7am slot and another doesn't, there's no way to see it. So whatever is working never spreads to the rest.

Profitmaxing is the whole job

Not bookings. Not app downloads. Profit per turf is the number we work on, and a full slot at the wrong price can still lose you money.

profit per turf = price × occupancy − running costs

Every decision has to move one of those three terms the right way without dragging the others down. Discounting into a full Saturday is a loss. Holding peak price on a dead Tuesday is also a loss. The work is knowing which hour is which.

  1. Measure

    Your bookings go in and real occupancy and revenue come out, split by hour, day and turf.

  2. Diagnose

    Separate the hours losing money on price from the ones losing it on demand. The fix is different for each.

  3. Fill

    Offers, reminders and calls aimed only at the hours that need them, sent to players who already book that time.

  4. Compare

    Put your turfs side by side, find the site doing something right, and run it everywhere.
